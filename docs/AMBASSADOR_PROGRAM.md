# Zipa Ambassador Program — Internal Operations Guide

## How to process applications

### 1. Review every application using a consistent scorecard
Score each applicant from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) on the following dimensions:

| Dimension | What to look for |
|---|---|
| **Relevance** | Does their background, location, or audience align with Zipa's target users in Nigeria? |
| **Communication** | Is their writing clear, persuasive, and free of hype or financial promises? |
| **Reliability** | Do they demonstrate consistency, follow-through, and professionalism? |
| **Audience / Community fit** | Is their stated audience real and engaged (not just bought followers)? |
| **Product curiosity** | Do they ask good questions or show genuine interest in how Zipa works? |
| **Integrity** | Do they avoid spammy language, guaranteed-return claims, or misleading statements? |

**Total score:** 30 points.

### 2. Shortlist approximately 25 people
Sort by total score. Invite the top ~25 to the next stage.

### 3. Interview or assess shortlisted candidates
Use a short call (10–15 min) or voice note (2–3 min) to verify:
- They understand Zipa is in testnet / early access.
- They can explain Zipa simply to a friend.
- Their availability matches the ~3 hours/week commitment.

### 4. Select 15–20 ambassadors and keep ~5 reserves
Send accepted applicants:
- Code of conduct
- Program dates and expectations
- Reward policy and onboarding schedule

### 5. Trial task
Give each approved ambassador a small, time-bound trial task before the official kickoff (e.g. "Explain Zipa to 3 friends and share their feedback").

### 6. Referral codes
Assign each ambassador a unique referral code or link for tracking.

### 7. Weekly activity review
Review every week based on:
- Quality of content or interactions
- Verified signups and activated users
- Useful product feedback
- Compliance with messaging rules

**Not** based on follower count alone.

### 8. Removal criteria
Remove ambassadors immediately for:
- Spam or fake referrals
- Misleading financial claims (guaranteed returns, token listings, real-money promises)
- Harassment or toxic behavior
- Misuse of user information

## Recommended 8-Week Program Schedule

| Week | Focus | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1** | Onboarding & training | Attend onboarding call, complete product quiz, set up referral code |
| **Week 2** | First touch | Introduce Zipa to your immediate network (5–10 people), collect feedback |
| **Week 3** | Content or event | Publish 1 piece of content or host 1 small meetup |
| **Week 4** | Mid-program check-in | Team call, review metrics, adjust track if needed |
| **Week 5** | Growth push | Drive 5+ verified signups or 1 merchant conversation |
| **Week 6** | Feedback loop | Submit detailed product feedback (bugs, UX, feature requests) |
| **Week 7** | Community building | Recruit or mentor 1–2 active community members |
| **Week 8** | Wrap-up & evaluation | Final report, celebrate top contributors, plan next cohort |

## Notes

- Ambassadors must never promise real-money availability, guaranteed returns, token listings, or specific rewards.
- All financial or regulatory questions must be redirected to `musa@usezipa.xyz`.
- Keep application data confidential. Do not share applicant details outside the review team.
